Virtuo

Where practice becomes performance.

Virtuo is an immersive performance-training experience designed for musicians, singers and performers.

Prepare for concerts, auditions and exams inside realistic virtual environments while using your real instrument through mixed reality passthrough.

Interact naturally through hand tracking, practise in different performance spaces and use guided mental preparation experiences before stepping onto the virtual stage.
