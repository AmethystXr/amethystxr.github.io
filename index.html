Featured project

Meet Virtuo.

An immersive performance-training experience for musicians, singers and performers.

Where practice becomes performance.

Prepare for concerts, auditions and exams inside realistic virtual spaces. Use your real instrument through mixed reality passthrough, interact naturally with hand tracking and perform before virtual audiences and examination commissions.

Guided backstage experiences support focus, presence and mental preparation before entering the virtual stage.
