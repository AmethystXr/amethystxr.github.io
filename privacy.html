Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Amethyst VR respects your privacy. This policy explains how Virtuo handles information when you use the application.

Information collection

Virtuo does not directly collect, transmit or store personal information. The application does not require an Amethyst VR account.

Device features

Virtuo may use Meta Quest features such as hand tracking, headset and controller tracking, and mixed reality passthrough to provide its core functionality. Processing associated with these features is performed by the device and the Meta platform in accordance with Meta's policies. Amethyst VR does not receive camera imagery from passthrough.

Subscriptions

Virtuo offers access through a subscription managed by the Meta Horizon platform. Meta processes subscription transactions and billing information. Virtuo may access subscription status information provided by Meta in order to determine whether a user has an active subscription and provide access to the corresponding features. Amethyst VR does not receive or store users' full payment-card details.

Third-party services

Virtuo does not sell personal information. Platform services required to install, subscribe to, update or run the application may be governed by Meta's own privacy terms.

Children

Virtuo is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Virtuo evolves. Any revised version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
